About Where Is My Sun

🌤️ Where Is My Sun helps bus passengers in Kerala choose the best seat to avoid direct sunlight during their journey.

✨ How it works:

• Select your starting point and destination

• The system analyzes the route direction and current time

• Get recommendations for which side of the bus to sit on to avoid sunlight

• During nighttime, enjoy comfortable seating without sun concerns

🚌 Travel smart and comfortably across Kerala!
